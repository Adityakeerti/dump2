# Nagar Nigam Dehradun Ward Governance and Emergency Helplines

## Administrative Structure
Nagar Nigam Dehradun is demarcated into 100 municipal wards grouped into 4 administrative zones (Central, North, South, and Trans-Rispana). Each ward is represented by an elected Ward Councillor (Parshad) and serviced by designated Sanitary Inspectors and maintenance squads.

## Key Municipal Zones and Prominent Wards
- Zone 1 (Central Zone): Ward 1 to Ward 25 (includes Clock Tower, Paltan Bazaar, Dhamawala, Chukkuwala, and Shivaji Dharamshala).
- Zone 2 (North Zone): Ward 26 to Ward 50 (includes Rajpur Road, Jakhan, Hathibarkala, Dalanwala, and Karanpur).
- Zone 3 (South Zone): Ward 51 to Ward 75 (includes Patel Nagar, Kargi, Majra, Niranjanpur, and Clement Town).
- Zone 4 (Trans-Rispana Zone): Ward 76 to Ward 100 (includes Rispana Nagar, Nehru Colony, Dharampur, and Chander Nagar).
