# Nagar Nigam Dehradun Commercial Trade License Issuance

## Purpose and Legal Requirement
Operating any commercial trade, business, restaurant, manufacturing unit, or service facility within the Nagar Nigam Dehradun municipal boundary requires a statutory Trade License issued under the Uttarakhand Municipal Corporation Act.

## Application Process
1. Online Filing: Log in to the municipal business portal https://nagarnigamdehradun.com and select 'Trade License Registration'.
2. Selection of Trade Category: Choose specific business type (retail general store, food establishment, pharmaceutical store, service center, or workshop).
3. Document Upload: Upload identity proof, premises ownership/lease, and relevant statutory approvals.
4. Physical Inspection: The area sanitary inspector conducts premises verification within 4 working days to verify safety and sanitation norms.
5. Fee Payment and License Delivery: Digital license with unique License Number and QR code is issued upon online fee payment.

## Required Documents
- Identity Proof: PAN Card and Aadhaar Card of the business proprietor or partners.
- Premises Proof: Rent Agreement / Registered Lease Deed or Municipal House Tax receipt if self-owned.
- NOC from Fire Department (mandatory for hotels, banquet halls, and premises exceeding 500 sq meters).
- FSSAI License copy (mandatory for restaurants, sweet shops, and food processing units).

## Validity and Renewal
Trade licenses remain valid for one financial year ending on 31st March. Annual renewal must be applied for between 1st April and 30th April to prevent a 25% late fee surcharge.
