# Nagar Nigam Dehradun Property Tax Self Assessment

## Overview
All residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional property owners located within the municipal jurisdiction of Nagar Nigam Dehradun are legally liable to submit annual Self-Assessment Property Tax (House Tax).

## Calculation Methodology
Property tax is assessed based on:
1. Covered Plinth Area: Total built-up square footage of each floor.
2. Construction Type: Pucca RCC roof, asbestos/tin shed, or semi-pucca structure.
3. Road Width: Property facing a major road (>24 feet) attracts higher circle rate factor compared to internal alleyways (<12 feet).
4. Usage Classification: Pure residential vs commercial/mixed use.

## Rebates and Penalties
- Early Bird Rebate: 10% rebate on current annual demand if paid before 30th September of the assessment financial year.
- Senior Citizen Rebate: Additional 5% rebate for self-occupied residential properties owned by citizens aged 65 and above.
- Late Penalty: Simple interest penalty of 12% per annum applies on overdue balances after 31st March.

## Online Payment Process
1. Visit https://nagarnigamdehradun.com and click on 'Online Property Tax'.
2. Enter Property ID / House Number or Mobile Number.
3. Review auto-populated plinth area, apply applicable rebate, and pay via Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI.
4. Download the instant digital tax receipt with official seal.
