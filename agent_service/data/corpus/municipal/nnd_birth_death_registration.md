# Nagar Nigam Dehradun Birth and Death Registration Procedure

## Statutory Framework
Registration of births and deaths is mandatory under the Registration of Births and Deaths (RBD) Act 1969. In urban areas of Dehradun, the Municipal Health Officer acts as the Registrar of Births and Deaths.

## Mandatory Reporting Timelines and Fees
- Normal Reporting (within 21 days): Free of cost. One complimentary copy of the certificate is issued.
- Delayed Reporting (22 to 30 days): Requires late fee of Rs 20 and written explanation to the registrar.
- Delayed Reporting (31 days to 1 year): Requires written permission from the District Registrar / SDM and late fee of Rs 50.
- Delayed Reporting (after 1 year): Requires an order from the First Class Executive Magistrate upon verifying non-availability certificate and local inquiry.

## Institutional vs Domiciliary (Home) Events
1. Institutional Events (Hospital, Nursing Home, Clinic):
   The hospital medical officer submits Form 1 (Birth) or Form 2 (Death) directly to the Civil Registration System (CRS) portal. Parents can download the digital certificate using the hospital reference slip.
2. Domiciliary Events (Home):
   The head of the family must report within 21 days with:
   - Proof of birth/death from local ward sanitary inspector or registered medical practitioner.
   - Aadhaar Cards of parents / deceased.
   - Address proof within Dehradun municipal limits.

## Online Verification Portal
Certificates with barcoded verification can be downloaded from the national portal https://crsorgi.gov.in or requested through Nagar Nigam Dehradun citizen facilitation counters.
