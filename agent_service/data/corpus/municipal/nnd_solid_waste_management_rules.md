# Nagar Nigam Dehradun Solid Waste Management and Door-to-Door Collection

## Overview
Under the Solid Waste Management Rules 2016 and Nagar Nigam Dehradun By-laws, all residential households, gated colonies, hotels, and commercial establishments must segregate waste at source and cooperate with authorized door-to-door waste collectors.

## Mandatory Waste Segregation Categories
1. Wet Waste (Green Bin): Organic kitchen waste, vegetable and fruit peels, food leftovers, tea leaves, and garden waste.
2. Dry Waste (Blue Bin): Paper, cardboard, milk pouches, dry plastic containers, glass bottles, and metal cans.
3. Domestic Hazardous Waste (Red Bin): Expired medicines, syringes, used diapers, sanitary napkins, tube lights, and paints.

## Collection Timings and User Charges
- Collection Hours: Door-to-door collection runs daily from 06:30 AM to 11:30 AM across all 100 municipal wards.
- Monthly Municipal User Charges:
  * Residential houses (<1000 sq ft): Rs 50 per month.
  * Residential houses (>1000 sq ft): Rs 100 per month.
  * Commercial shops / offices: Rs 200 per month.
  * Restaurants / Hotels: Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 depending on waste volume.

## Penalties for Violations
- Open burning of dry waste or leaves: Rs 5,000 instant spot fine.
- Littering in drains or public roadways: Rs 500 for first offence, Rs 2,000 for repeated offence.
- Non-segregation of waste handed to municipal tippers: Refusal of collection and warning notice.
