# Nagar Nigam Dehradun Civic Grievance Redressal and SLAs

## Overview
Nagar Nigam Dehradun (NND), located at Court Road, Dehradun, is the urban local government responsible for municipal civil works, sanitation, public lighting, and health services across 100 wards.

## Authorized Civic Complaint Categories
Citizens can lodge grievances under the following defined municipal categories:
1. Potholes and Road Damage: Major potholes on municipal internal roads, broken interlocking tiles, and sunken road chambers.
2. Defective Streetlights: Non-functional LED streetlights, flickering fixtures, dangling electric cables, or dark road stretches.
3. Garbage and Sanitation Dumps: Overflowing public dustbins, uncollected roadside waste heaps, and dead animal removal.
4. Blocked Drains and Sewage Overflow: Choked stormwater drains, overflowing drainage chambers, and open manhole covers.
5. Encroachments and Unauthorized Construction: Obstruction of public footpaths, pedestrian walkways, and unauthorized hoardings.

## Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for Resolution
- Hazardous open manholes: Emergency action within 6 hours.
- Defective streetlight repair: Resolution within 24 to 48 hours.
- Garbage dump cleaning: Resolution within 24 hours.
- Internal municipal road pothole patching: 7 working days.
- Stray animal control and dog catching: 48 hours.

## Departmental Escalation Ladder
If a complaint is not resolved within the defined SLA, the grievance state machine automatically escalates through tiers:
- Level 1: Field Worker / Junior Engineer (JE).
- Level 2: Ward Sanitary Inspector or Maintenance In-charge.
- Level 3: Municipal Health Officer (MHO) or Assistant Municipal Commissioner.
- Level 4: Additional Municipal Commissioner.
- Level 5: Municipal Commissioner (Nagar Aayukt), Nagar Nigam Dehradun.
- Final Level: State Chief Minister Grievance Portal (CM Helpline 1905).

## Grievance Lodging Channels
Citizens can register grievances via the Nagar Nigam portal https://nagarnigamdehradun.com, via the CitySense Ask/Report tab, or call the NND Control Room toll-free at 1800-180-4151.
