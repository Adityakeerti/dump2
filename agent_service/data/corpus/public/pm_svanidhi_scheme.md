# PM SVANidhi Micro-Credit Scheme for Street Vendors

## Scheme Summary
PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi) is a central sector scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to facilitate affordable working capital loans to urban street vendors whose livelihoods were impacted by economic disruptions.

## Tranche Loans and Financial Benefits
- 1st Tranche: Initial working capital loan of up to Rs 10,000 with a 1-year repayment tenure.
- 2nd Tranche: Enhanced working capital loan of up to Rs 20,000 upon timely repayment of the first tranche.
- 3rd Tranche: Working capital loan of up to Rs 50,000 for vendors with excellent digital repayment history.
- Interest Subsidy: An interest subsidy of 7% per annum is credited directly to the vendor's bank account on timely quarterly repayment.
- Digital Cashback: Monthly cashback incentives up to Rs 100 for digital payments and receipts via UPI QR codes.

## Eligibility Conditions
1. Vendors engaged in vending in urban areas on or before the qualifying cut-off date.
2. Possession of a Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Identity Card issued by the Urban Local Body (Nagar Nigam Dehradun).
3. Vendors left out of the formal vending survey can obtain a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from the municipal town vending committee.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory KYC document).
- Voter Identity Card or Driving License.
- Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Letter of Recommendation (LoR) issued by Nagar Nigam Dehradun.
- Active savings bank account linked to an Aadhaar-seeded mobile number.

## Application Channels
Vendors can apply through the official web portal https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in or via the PM SVANidhi mobile application or through banking correspondents at nationalized banks in Dehradun.
