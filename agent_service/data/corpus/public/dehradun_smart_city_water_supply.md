# Dehradun Smart City Water Supply and Smart Metering

## System Description
Dehradun Smart City Limited (DSCL) in coordination with Uttarakhand Peyjal Nigam and Jal Sansthan operates the smart 24x7 water distribution network covering core city zones including Rajpur Road, Clock Tower, Paltan Bazaar, and Dalanwala.

## New Domestic Water Connection Procedure
1. Eligibility: Property owners and registered tenants with landlord consent within statutory municipal boundaries of Dehradun.
2. Submission of Application: Submit Form W-1 online via the municipal smart city portal https://smartcitydehradun.uk.gov.in or at the Jal Sansthan division office at Dalanwala.
3. Site Inspection: A junior engineer inspects the property within 3 working days to check pipeline feasibility and pipeline distance.
4. Estimate and Connection: Upon payment of connection fee and meter deposit, the connection is commissioned within 7 working days.

## Documents Required
- Property Tax receipt (latest paid receipt from Nagar Nigam Dehradun).
- Ownership deed, Registry copy, or Valid rent agreement.
- Aadhaar Card of the applicant.
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) if pipeline crosses private shared passage.

## Billing and Grievance Redressal
- Water billing is calculated on AMR (Automated Meter Reading) smart water meters based on slab tariff.
- Bill payments can be completed online via the Dehradun Smart City portal.
- For pipeline bursts, low pressure, or contaminated water complaints, residents can contact the 24x7 toll-free helpline 1800-180-2525.
