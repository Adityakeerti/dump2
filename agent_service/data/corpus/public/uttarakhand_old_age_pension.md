# Uttarakhand Social Welfare Old Age Pension Scheme

## Scheme Overview
The Department of Social Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand, provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens under the State Old Age Pension Scheme to ensure economic self-reliance and dignified living.

## Benefit Amount
- Beneficiaries receive Rs 1,500 per month deposited directly into their Aadhaar-seeded bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Eligibility Criteria
1. The applicant must be a permanent resident of Uttarakhand residing in districts including Dehradun.
2. The applicant must have attained 60 years of age or older.
3. The applicant's monthly family income must not exceed Rs 4,000 in urban areas, or the applicant must be listed in the BPL (Below Poverty Line) list.
4. The applicant must not be a recipient of any other central, state, or defense pension.

## Required Documents
- Proof of Age: High School Certificate, Voter ID, or Medical Age Certificate issued by the Chief Medical Officer (CMO).
- Resident / Domicile Certificate of Uttarakhand.
- Family Income Certificate issued by the competent Tehsildar or Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM).
- Aadhaar Card.
- Bank Account Passbook (single account in a nationalized bank with IFSC code).
- Two passport-sized photographs.

## Application Portal
Applications can be submitted online via the integrated social security portal https://ssp.uk.gov.in or offline through the District Social Welfare Office (DSWO) located at Court Road, Dehradun.
