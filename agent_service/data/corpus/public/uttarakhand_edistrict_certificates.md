# Uttarakhand e-District Resident and Income Certificates

## Service Overview
The e-District Uttarakhand mission provides citizens of Dehradun transparent and time-bound delivery of statutory revenue certificates through https://edistrict.uk.gov.in under the Uttarakhand Right to Service Act.

## Domicile / Permanent Resident Certificate (Mool Niwas)
- Eligibility: Citizen must be residing in Uttarakhand continuously for at least 15 years, or born in Uttarakhand with ancestral immovable property.
- Required Documents:
  * Land Registry / Khatauni or Nagar Nigam municipal property tax receipt showing residential address.
  * Continuous 15-year school education records / certificates in Uttarakhand.
  * Aadhaar Card and Parivar Register copy from the local ward or village council.
- Service Delivery Timeline (SLA): 15 working days.

## Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra)
- Eligibility: Any head of household requiring certified proof of annual income for scholarship, pension, or housing benefits.
- Required Documents:
  * Salary slip / Form 16 (for salaried persons) or Self-Declaration affidavit notarized (for self-employed / laborers).
  * Bank account statement for the past 6 months.
  * Aadhaar Card.
- Service Delivery Timeline (SLA): 7 working days.

## Application and Verification Flow
Applicants apply online through the e-District citizen portal or visit any CSC Jan Seva Kendra. Applications undergo field inquiry by the area Lekhpal / Patwari before final digital signature by the Tehsildar or SDM. Certified PDFs with QR codes can be downloaded directly from the portal.
