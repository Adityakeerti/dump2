# Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

## Scheme Description
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government-funded healthcare assurance scheme, administered by the National Health Authority (NHA). It provides health insurance cover to vulnerable urban and rural families across India.

## Health Benefit Package
- Cover Amount: Cashless health cover of up to Rs 5,00,000 per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
- Pre and Post Hospitalization: Covers diagnostic investigations, medications, and room charges up to 3 days prior and 15 days post discharge.
- Pre-existing Diseases: All pre-existing medical conditions are covered from day one of card activation.
- Family Size: No cap on family size, age, or gender.

## Eligibility
- Identification based on Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) deprivation criteria for urban occupational categories (ragpickers, domestic workers, sanitation workers, transport workers, and small shop helpers).
- Beneficiary status can be checked by searching mobile number, ration card number, or Aadhaar on https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in.

## Documentation Required for Ayushman Card Generation
- Individual Aadhaar Card with biometric or OTP authentication.
- NFSA Ration Card (White or Yellow) or official state family register copy.
- Active mobile phone for OTP verification.

## Hospital Empanelment and Claim Process
Patients can present their Golden Ayushman Card at any empanelled public hospital (e.g., Doon Medical College Hospital, Dehradun) or empanelled private specialty hospital. The Ayushman Mitra desk verifies the card and processes cashless admission directly without out-of-pocket deposits.
