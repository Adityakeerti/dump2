# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Urban (PMAY-U)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) is a flagship mission by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), Government of India, that provides all-weather pucca houses to eligible urban households across all statutory towns including Dehradun, Uttarakhand.

## Beneficiary Categories and Annual Income Criteria
The scheme covers four distinct income categories:
- Economically Weaker Section (EWS): Annual household income up to Rs 3,00,000.
- Low Income Group (LIG): Annual household income between Rs 3,00,001 and Rs 6,00,000.
- Middle Income Group I (MIG-I): Annual household income between Rs 6,00,001 and Rs 12,00,000.
- Middle Income Group II (MIG-II): Annual household income between Rs 12,00,001 and Rs 18,00,000.

## Mandatory Eligibility Criteria
1. The beneficiary family must not own a pucca house in their name or in the name of any family member anywhere in India.
2. A beneficiary family consists of husband, wife, unmarried sons, and unmarried daughters.
3. Houses constructed under the scheme must be registered in the name of the female head of the family or jointly with the male head.

## Required Documents
Applicants must provide the following verified documents:
- Aadhaar Card of all family members.
- Permanent Address Proof (Voter ID, Electricity Bill, or Ration Card).
- Bank Account Details with IFSC code (linked to Aadhaar for DBT transfer).
- Income Certificate issued by competent tehsildar or revenue authority.
- Affidavit declaring no ownership of any pucca house across India.
- Land ownership title (patta or registered deed) for Beneficiary-Led Construction (BLC).

## Application Procedure
Eligible residents can apply online via the official portal https://pmaymis.gov.in or in person at the Dehradun Nagar Nigam Common Service Center (CSC). No middleman fee is required.
