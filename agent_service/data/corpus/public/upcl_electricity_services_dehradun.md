# UPCL Electricity Connection and Consumer Grievances Dehradun

## Overview
Uttarakhand Power Corporation Limited (UPCL) is the sole electricity distribution utility serving consumers across the Dehradun metropolitan area and surrounding urban circles.

## Applying for a New Domestic Electricity Connection
Citizens can submit applications under the '1 Stop Portal' at https://www.upcl.org for domestic load sanctions:
1. Online Application: Fill consumer details, select sanctioned load (standard domestic 1 kW to 5 kW), and upload documents.
2. Demand Note Generation: Demand notice is generated within 48 hours following document verification.
3. Meter Installation: Smart electricity meter is installed within 5 working days after security deposit and service line fee payment.

## Required Documents
- Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card or Voter Identity Card.
- Proof of Ownership or Occupancy: Municipal property tax receipt, sale deed, or registered lease agreement.
- Test Report: Internal electrical wiring test report certified by a licensed electrical contractor.
