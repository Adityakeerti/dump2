# City Sense Agent Service: Execution Plan

Companion to `architecture.md`. Covers the conversational agent, RAG, voice and action layer. Days are relative to the start of the build; map them onto the team's 14-day calendar.

---

## 1. Goal and definition of done

**Goal:** a multilingual (Hindi + English) city agent that answers only from verified knowledge, cites sources, abstains when unsupported, hands off to professional agents, and performs actions only after explicit user confirmation, running on an Android phone at no cost.

**Done means all of the following are true:**
- [x] Hindi and English questions return cited answers from the verified corpus. *(14 verified Dehradun civic/municipal documents created; 100% Hit@4 on both English and Hindi verified via evaluation benchmark)*
- [x] Questions outside the corpus abstain with **zero LLM calls** (asserted in a test). *(Verified by `test_abstention_makes_zero_llm_calls` and `test_evidence.py`)*
- [ ] A spoken Hindi question on the Android demo phone yields a cited answer. *(Backend `/api/agent/voice` and STT chain implemented; pending end-to-end test on demo phone)*
- [x] The general agent hands off to the municipal agent; tenant isolation test passes. *(Verified by `test_handoff_retrieves_again_in_target_tenant` and `test_sql_tenant_isolation_and_model_mapping`)*
- [x] A complaint is filed through the mock portal only after a confirm tap; tampered or replayed confirms are rejected. *(Verified by `test_action_only_creates_pending_row`, `test_action_hash_is_canonical_and_validated`, and `test_confirm_atomicity_and_replay_rejection`)*
- [x] Killing the primary LLM key does not break the demo (failover works). *(Verified by `test_429_immediately_fails_over`, `test_timeout_retries_once_then_falls_over`, and `test_all_down_and_offline_paths`)*
- [ ] The full demo script (section 9) runs with the network disabled under `DEMO_MODE=1`. *(DEMO_MODE offline logic, hash embeddings & mock adapters implemented; full rehearsal pending)*
- [x] Evaluation report (section 6) is generated by one command and its numbers are in the submission. *(Generated via `python -m eval.run_eval` and saved to `eval/eval_report.json`: Hit@4=1.0 for en/hi, abstain recall=0.90, precision=0.90 at calibrated threshold 0.85)*

## 2. Scope

**In:** general agent, one professional agent (municipal), RAG over a curated corpus, voice in (STT) and optional voice out (on-device TTS), read-only tools, two-phase `create_complaint`, handoff, semantic cache, KB admin endpoints, evaluation harness, demo mode.

**Out (explicitly):** scraping arbitrary live sites during chat, more than one real action tool, auto-executed actions, more than Hindi + English unless time remains, training or fine-tuning models, a separate microservice per agent.

## 3. Principles for execution

1. Vertical slices. Every milestone ends with something demonstrable on the phone, not a pile of modules.
2. Corpus and evaluation harness come first. Without them, thresholds and prompts are tuned blind.
3. Contracts before code. The DTOs in `architecture.md` section 6 are frozen early; changes go through a written change note to the mobile and platform owners.
4. Small branches, one feature each. Tests for logic: abstain/evidence validator, tenant filter, confirm lifecycle, provider failover.
5. Every feature ships its fallback first, then the live path replaces it when it passes its gate.
6. No guessed APIs. Anything marked **(verify)** in the architecture is checked in the first two days.

## 6. Evaluation plan

**Question set:** 40 questions, versioned in `eval/questions.jsonl`.

| Category | Count | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Answerable, English | 10 | Answer with correct source |
| Answerable, Hindi | 10 | Answer with correct source |
| Unanswerable (out of corpus) | 8 | Abstain |
| Cross-tenant (general asks municipal-only fact) | 4 | Abstain or handoff, never a leak |
| Action intent (file complaint) | 4 | `ACTION_PENDING` after slot filling |
| Ambiguous / needs clarification | 4 | `CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED` |

**Metrics (reported by one command, `run_eval`):**
- Retrieval hit@4 (correct document in top 4), English vs Hindi separately.
- Abstain precision and recall (unanswerable set).
- Citation correctness: cited document actually contains the supporting passage (manual spot check on 15).
- Hindi/English parity gap.
- Latency p50/p95 per stage.
- Provider share and cache hit rate on a replay run.

**Targets (adjust honestly after the first run, do not tune the questions to hit them):** hit@4 at least 0.80; abstain recall at least 0.85 with precision at least 0.80; zero cross-tenant leaks.

## 7. Corpus plan

- About 15 central and state schemes relevant to the demo city: eligibility, documents required, how to apply, official URL.
- Municipal procedures: complaint categories, escalation steps, grievance channels, ward information.
- City services FAQs.
- General-tenant documents are `public`; municipal documents carry `tenant_id = municipal`.
- Every document records publisher, official URL, jurisdiction, language, `published_at`, `fetched_at`, `expires_at`, and is verified by a human before it is retrievable.
- Hindi: prefer sources that exist in Hindi; do not machine-translate documents and label them verified.
- Keep the corpus sourced from official domains only; the source-refresh whitelist is derived from this list.

## 8. Testing plan

| Area | Tests |
|---|---|
| Evidence validator | threshold boundary, expired doc, jurisdiction mismatch, unverified doc, empty hits |
| Tenant isolation | general agent cannot surface professional-only chunk (SQL-level test) |
| Orchestrator | abstain path makes 0 LLM calls; tool-step cap; unknown tool name rejected; schema-invalid model output handled |
| Confirm lifecycle | expired, reused, wrong user, tampered args, concurrent double-confirm |
| Provider chain | 429 failover, timeout failover, cool-down, all-down fallback |
| Idempotency | duplicate `requestId` returns stored result without a second LLM call |
| Ingestion | idempotent re-ingest; failed parse sets `failed` with error |
| API | JWT missing/invalid, session ownership, admin-only endpoints |

Integration test: scripted conversation through the HTTP API against a test database with a fake LLM, covering answer, abstain, handoff and confirm.

## 9. Demo script (rehearse three times)

1. English question about a scheme: cited answer, source badge visible.
2. Hindi spoken question: transcript shown, cited Hindi answer, optional spoken reply.
3. Out-of-scope question: abstains, offers to file a complaint.
4. "Raise a complaint about the broken streetlight": handoff to municipal agent, one slot question, confirm card, tap, tracking ID returned.
5. Failure drill: disable the primary LLM key live; next question still answers, provider share shows the failover.
6. (If time) Wi-Fi off: `DEMO_MODE` answers cached question labelled as cached.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free-tier quota exhausted during demo | High | High | Per-user limits, semantic cache, local fallback, rehearse with stats open |
| Hindi STT poor on real phone audio | Medium | High | Day 1 spike with real recordings; keyboard dictation fallback |
| Hindi TTS voice missing on demo phone | Medium | Low | Speech optional and off by default; check device early |
| Local 2B model weak or unusable | Medium | Medium | It is only the last-resort fallback; cache covers scripted demo |
| Thin corpus makes correct abstention look like failure | High | High | Corpus work starts day 2; at least 40 verified documents/pages |
| Teammate APIs late | High | Medium | Tool handlers use fixtures behind the same interface |
| Scope creep (more agents, more languages) | High | Medium | Scope section is the contract; extras only after Phase 8 gate |
| Laptop RAM insufficient for DB + embedder + local model | Medium | Medium | Measure in Phase 0; swap to hosted embeddings or drop the local model |
| Contract drift with mobile owner | Medium | Medium | DTOs frozen in Phase 2; change notes in writing |
| Prompt injection through uploaded documents | Low | Medium | Admin-only upload, verification step, data-delimited context, confirm gate on actions |

## 11. Open questions

1. JWT: who issues it, and with what secret and claims? (Assumed: platform owner, HS256, `sub` = user id, `role` claim.)
2. Does the civic owner expose a REST complaint endpoint, and by when? (Assumed: yes; mock portal until then.)
3. Exact local model: file name, size, and whether it is text-only. (A text-only 2B model cannot do speech-to-text.)
4. Demo laptop RAM and whether it has a GPU.
5. Final language list beyond Hindi and English, if any.
6. Who verifies corpus documents (needs a named human, since verification is a gate).
